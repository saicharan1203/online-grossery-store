Ultra Smooth Scrolling

Experience 120fps-quality scrolling with buttery smooth animations. Cinematic, effortless, and premium — just like Apple.

Feature 01

Hardware Acceleration

Every animation uses GPU-accelerated transforms and opacity. No layout reflows, no janky frames — just pure smoothness powered by transform3d and will-change optimization.

Feature 02

Smooth Interpolation

Custom requestAnimationFrame-based scrolling with LERP (linear interpolation). Every frame is perfectly timed, creating a continuous, fluid motion that feels premium and controlled.

Feature 03

Perfect Sync

Scroll-triggered animations stay perfectly synced with your scroll position. No skipped frames, no lag — animations appear exactly when they should, creating a cinematic storytelling experience.

Feature 04

High Refresh Rate

Optimized for 120Hz displays with stable frame pacing. Ultra-responsive to mouse wheel, trackpad, and touch inputs. Feel the difference on high-refresh-rate monitors.
